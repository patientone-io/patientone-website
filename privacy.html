Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

At patientone labs ("we", "us", "our"), accessible from https://patientone.uk, we believe privacy is an architectural principle, not an afterthought. We build tools that run locally whenever possible and do not treat your private data as a commodity.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website and our browser extensions published on the Chrome Web Store and other distribution platforms.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or monetize your personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not track your browsing history across unrelated websites.
  • We do not inject third-party analytics, behavioral tracking scripts, or ad networks into our browser extensions.
  • We do not transmit your clipboard contents or keystrokes to external tracking servers.

3. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) & AI Processing

For tools and extensions featuring artificial intelligence or LLM integrations, we employ a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) or client-directed architecture:

  • Local API Key Storage: Your API keys (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter) are stored exclusively on your device using the browser's native secure local storage (chrome.storage.local). They are never transmitted to or stored on patientone labs servers.
  • Direct Communication: Prompts, queries, and content you request to be processed are sent directly from your browser client to the API endpoint of the provider you configured. Your interactions are governed by that provider's respective privacy policy.

4. Permissions in Browser Extensions

Our extensions request only the minimal set of browser permissions strictly necessary to execute their explicit features:

  • storage: To save your configuration, preferences, and local API keys on your machine.
  • activeTab / contextMenus: Used strictly when you trigger an action on an active page (e.g., analyzing page text or checking availability).

5. Payments & Commercial Transactions

When you purchase licenses or subscriptions for our software, payments are processed by an authorized Merchant of Record (e.g., LemonSqueezy, Paddle, or Stripe). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers or financial credentials on our servers. The payment processor handles billing information in full compliance with PCI-DSS and GDPR regulations.

6. GDPR & User Rights

Under European data protection laws (GDPR), users have the right to access, rectify, or erase their personal data. Because our tools operate with local-first architectures and do not maintain user databases on central servers, you retain full autonomy over your data by clearing your browser's local storage or uninstalling the extension.
