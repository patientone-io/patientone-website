# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People interested in patientone's open-source homelab, Linux, audio and browser tools. They need to understand each tool and reach its repository.

## Product Purpose

A personal, bilingual portfolio presenting tools patientone builds and uses. Success means a visitor understands the work and can open a project or contact the author.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the four current projects: Jellyfin Checker, DiunTap, opusbook and legion-nvidia-off; public contact, GitHub links, Polish/English switching, privacy and terms links. The existing implementation is static HTML/CSS/JavaScript with self-hosted fonts and no build step.

## Brand Commitments

Name: patientone. One person, first-person voice, plain and factual PL/EN. Do not invent company positioning, testimonials, usage figures or product capabilities. Visual identity: graphite background, warm white humanist sans, mineral mint links, a clear introduction, four compact project rows with small icons and technology tags, and a direct contact close. Each project row is one native repository link. The avatar is part of the shared header identity and remains the social preview image.

## Evidence on Hand

Current project descriptions and links in index.html; avatar.png; privacy.html; terms.html. Design tokens and component behavior are documented in DESIGN.md.

## Product Principles

- Let real projects explain the author's work.
- Keep navigation to code and contact direct.
- Preserve natural Polish and English.
- Prefer legibility and useful interaction to decoration.
