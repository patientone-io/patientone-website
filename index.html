Philosophy

How we engineer software

01

Intent-Driven Velocity

By leveraging autonomous AI orchestration and rapid vibe-coding paradigms, we move from conceptual idea to production-grade utility in hours, skipping bureaucratic bloat.

02

Architectural Privacy (BYOK)

We reject the surveillance business model. Whenever possible, our tools use the Bring-Your-Own-Key model: your API tokens remain in your browser storage and connect directly to providers.

03

Single-Purpose Precision

No messy multi-tools or confusing menus. Each extension solves one acute problem with uncompromising speed, rock-solid stability, and minimal memory footprint.
